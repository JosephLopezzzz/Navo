# Additional Navo tones

Clear Signal, Gentle Rise, and Steady Beacon are original deterministic sine-wave compositions generated by `scripts/generate-additional-alarm-tones.py`. They contain no sampled, downloaded, or phone-manufacturer audio. Their source is this repository's generator; no external sound attribution is required.

Each is approximately one second, mono PCM16 WAV at 16 kHz. Together the three source files occupy under 100 KiB. They are suitable for short notification cues, rather than continuous sirens.

On Android, **Phone alarm** and **Choose from phone alarm sounds** reference Android's existing alarm sound URIs without copying their audio into Navo. The available list depends on the device and permissions; Navo requests no extra media/storage permission for this list. Sounds removed or made inaccessible by the OS fall back to the notification default. The native implementation uses `RingtoneManager.TYPE_ALARM`, and notifications use `AudioAttributes.USAGE_ALARM`; phone volume, notification channel settings, Do Not Disturb, and vendor policies still apply.

iOS does not expose the Clock app's tone library through Navo. It uses the bundled tones, System default, and imported sounds. iOS notification vibration follows system notification settings; Android's Navo vibration choice uses separate immutable sound/vibration channels.

References: [Android RingtoneManager](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/RingtoneManager), [NotificationChannel sound](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/NotificationChannel#setSound(android.net.Uri,%20android.media.AudioAttributes)), [Expo SDK58 notifications](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v58.0.0/sdk/notifications/).
